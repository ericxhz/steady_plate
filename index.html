Why food patterns matter

More steady. Less complicated.

You do not need a “perfect” diet. A few repeatable patterns can support long-term health. Food is one part of prevention, alongside movement and appropriate medical care.

01

Fiber first

Vegetables, beans, whole grains, nuts, and whole fruit add fiber and staying power.

02

Pair your carbs

Enjoy carbohydrates with protein, fiber, or healthy fat to make a meal more satisfying.

03

Build consistency

Small choices you can repeat matter more than dramatic changes that do not last.
